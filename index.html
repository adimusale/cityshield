Critical Arrival Advisory

New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS)

ALWAYS EXIT VIA AJMERI GATE (Platform 16 Side):

The Ajmeri Gate exit features an air-conditioned skywalk connecting directly to the Delhi Metro Yellow Line and Airport Express, plus an official Delhi Police prepaid auto booth.

DANGER ON PAHARGANJ SIDE (Platform 1):

Aggressive touts approach newcomers claiming: "All hotels in Paharganj are sealed/curfewed" or pose as government tourism agents. Never board unmetered autos waiting on the street!

Safe Transit Options

  • • Delhi Metro: Yellow Line & Airport Express Line directly under station.
  • • Delhi Police Prepaid Auto: Fixed fare slip counter at exit.
  • • Uber/Ola: Dedicated parking pillar pickup.

Anti-Scam Golden Rule #1

Never Pay A Single Rupee Before Visiting

Fraudsters on OLX/Instagram claim to be Army Officers asking for ₹1,500 "Society Gate Passes" or "Viewing Tokens". Genuine landlords NEVER charge an advance fee to show a room.

10-Point Pre-Signing Room Inspection Checklist

1. Electricity Unit Rate (Most Common Trap): Ask: "Is electricity charged at domestic government slab (~₹7-8) or commercial ₹12-16/unit?" Get the exact per-unit price written on the agreement.
2. Deposit Refund Timeline: Ensure agreement clearly states the deposit will be returned within 7 days of moving out via bank transfer, with deductions only for actual unpaid bills.
3. Notice Period Policy: Standard is 30 days. Avoid landlords who demand 60 or 90 days notice, or who state deposits are non-refundable during the first 6 months.
4. Day-1 Video Evidence: On the day you take keys, record a continuous 3-minute video showing wall condition, fan, taps, and mirror. Send it to the landlord on WhatsApp as proof.

Phase 4

Anti-Fraud Shield & Blacklist

Unmasking touts, fake brokers, unmetered autos, and advance fee scams in Delhi NCR.

Critical Threat

The "Visiting Pass / Gate Entry Pass" Scam

Scammer poses as Army Officer/NRI owner on OLX, asking for ₹1,500–₹2,000 for a "refundable digital gate pass" via QR code before showing the room. They block you immediately after payment.

Defense Rule: NEVER transfer money before meeting physically inside the room.
Critical Threat

The "Hotel Closed / Curfewed" Station Scam

Touts outside New Delhi Station claim your booked hotel burned down or is under police seal, steering you to partner hotels charging ₹5,000/night for dingy rooms.

Defense Rule: Call the hotel yourself from your own phone. Walk directly to Delhi Metro.
High Threat

Sub-Meter Electricity Overcharge Trap

PG owner charges ₹14 to ₹16 per unit on domestic sub-meters instead of government ₹6-8 rate, resulting in unexpected ₹5,000 summer electricity bills.

Defense Rule: Fix unit cost explicitly in the written agreement before paying security deposit.
Critical Threat

"Hotel Review / Telegram Task" Part-Time Job Scam

Newcomer receives WhatsApp message offering ₹3,000/day for liking YouTube videos. Scammer pays ₹200 initially, then demands ₹10,000 "prepaid investment".

Defense Rule: Genuine companies never ask you to pay money to earn salary. Dial 1930 immediately.
